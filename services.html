Services

What MeNI actually does.

Four services, all built around the same idea: you should end up in the right property, understanding why it is the right one.

Start an enquiry

01 — Student accommodation

Accommodation around UNN, without the runaround.

Finding a place near the University of Nigeria, Nsukka usually means walking the area, chasing numbers and hoping the room is still available when you arrive.

We do that part. Tell us your budget, the area you need to be in and when you need to move, and we come back with options that exist and are available.

Good to know

  • Tell us your budget honestly — it saves everyone time
  • Proximity, water, power and security matter as much as price
  • Ask early. Options thin out as the session approaches
  • We will tell you when an option is not worth your money

What we need from you

  • Type of property and how it will be used
  • Preferred area or areas
  • Budget range
  • When you need to move
  • Anything that is non-negotiable

02 — Property sourcing

You describe it. We go and find it.

Property sourcing is search work done properly: understanding the requirement, looking in the right places, and verifying what we find before we bring it to you.

You get a shortlist with the trade-offs explained. If nothing suitable exists at your budget, we tell you that instead of showing you something that doesn't fit.

03 — Property owner services

Your property, presented as it deserves.

Most properties are let on a photo taken in a hurry and a phone number passed around. That attracts volume, not the right occupant.

We present your property properly, screen the interest it attracts, coordinate viewings and keep you informed. We do not promise guaranteed occupancy — we promise the work that makes occupancy more likely.

See the full offer for property owners

Where we add value

  • Presentation that shows the property accurately
  • Prospective occupants who are genuinely searching
  • Viewings coordinated around your availability
  • One point of contact, clear updates

Questions worth asking first

  • What does this location cost me in time, every day?
  • What am I actually paying for beyond the rent?
  • What would make this property hard to leave later?
  • Is the paperwork what it appears to be?

04 — Property consultation

Think it through before the money moves.

Sometimes the most valuable thing we do is help someone decide not to proceed.

Consultation is a conversation about the decision in front of you: what the option gives you, what it costs you, and what you should confirm before committing.
